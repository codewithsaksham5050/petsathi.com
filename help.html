Help & FAQ

Everything you need to know about using petsath.com

How do I list my pet for adoption?

Register with the "Pet Owner" role, go to your Profile page, open the "My Pets" tab and click "Add Pet". You must upload at least 3 photos of the pet.

How do I adopt a pet?

Register with the "Pet Adopter" role, browse the Gallery, open a pet's details page and click "Request Adoption". The owner and our admin team will review your request.

Can I message the pet's owner before adopting?

Yes. On any pet's details page click "Chat with Owner" to ask questions directly before requesting adoption.

What does "liking" a pet do?

Liking bookmarks a pet so you can find it again quickly from your profile, and lets the owner know there's interest.

Who approves adoption requests?

Our admin team reviews every adoption request submitted through the platform to keep the process safe and fair for everyone.

Is petsath.com free to use?

Yes, registering, listing pets, and adopting are completely free.

I still need help — who do I contact?

Head over to our Contact page and send us a message — we usually reply within a day.
